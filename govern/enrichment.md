# Enrichment

Enrichers allow you to enhance and improve the quality and completeness of your golden records by incorporating additional information from third-party sources.

The following diagram shows the basic steps of enriching the data in CluedIn.

![How to enrich your data in CluedIn: 1 choose records for enrichment, 2 add and configure an enricher, 3 review enriched records for new properties](https://documentation.cluedin.net/assets/images/preparation/enricher/enricher-intro.gif)

This section covers the following areas:

- [Concept of enricher](https://documentation.cluedin.net/govern/enrichment/concept-of-enricher.md) – explore how an enricher works and where it fits in the data life cycle in CluedIn.

- [Adding an enricher](https://documentation.cluedin.net/govern/enrichment/add-enricher.md) – learn how to configure and manage an enricher.

- [Reference information about enrichers](https://documentation.cluedin.net/govern/enrichment/enricher-reference.md) – find information about built-in enrichers available in CluedIn.

    If you need a custom enricher, use the [REST API enricher](https://documentation.cluedin.net/govern/enrichment/rest-api.md) or contact us, and we will build one for you.
