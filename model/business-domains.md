# Business domains

A business domain says what kind of thing a record is - Customer, Product, Organization. It drives how records are searched, displayed, resolved and exported.

Business domains were called *entity types* before the 2025.05 release.

## In this section

- [Business domains explained](https://documentation.cluedin.net/model/business-domains/business-domains-explained.md) (concept): A business domain represents a specific business object that describes the semantic meaning of golden records.
- [Manage business domains](https://documentation.cluedin.net/model/business-domains/manage-business-domains.md) (how-to): A business domain is a well-known business object that describes the semantic meaning of golden records.
