# Project delivery

How to scope and run a CluedIn project: what to decide before you start, how to sequence the work, and what has to be true before you go to production.

## In this section

- [Before you start](https://documentation.cluedin.net/playbooks/project-delivery/before-you-start.md) (concept): Before you start implementing your CluedIn project, take a moment to read this page and get to know us better.
- [How to approach your CluedIn project](https://documentation.cluedin.net/playbooks/project-delivery/how-to-approach-your-cluedin-project.md) (concept): This article outlines two fundamental steps for approaching any data project in CluedIn.
- [Prepare for your CluedIn project](https://documentation.cluedin.net/playbooks/project-delivery/prepare-for-your-cluedin-project.md) (concept): This article outlines four key steps to prepare for the implementation of you project in CluedIn.
- [Start your CluedIn project](https://documentation.cluedin.net/playbooks/project-delivery/start-your-cluedin-project.md) (concept): This article outlines the main steps for starting your CluedIn project.
- [Start your IT journey](https://documentation.cluedin.net/playbooks/project-delivery/start-your-it-journey.md) (concept): This article outlines the key stages of your IT journey, from installing CluedIn and integrating it with necessary external systems and sources, to ensuring that user access aligns with your organization policies.
- [Release to production playbook](https://documentation.cluedin.net/playbooks/project-delivery/release-to-production-playbook.md) (concept): Now that you have implemented your first use case in the test environment, it is time to release your solution to production.
- [Example RACI matrix](https://documentation.cluedin.net/playbooks/project-delivery/raci-example.md) (reference): That includes platform installation, technical onboarding, ingestion setup, data-model implementation, rule configuration, dedup/survivorship setup, export/stream/API enablement, training, handover, and hypercare.
