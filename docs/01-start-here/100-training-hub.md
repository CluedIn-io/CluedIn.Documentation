Training is an in-app area where you complete short lessons - videos and guides, organized into categories and modules - and automatically earn certifications once you finish everything a certification requires. Use it to learn the platform without leaving CluedIn; use the [written documentation](/) when you need the full detail behind a specific feature.

## Prerequisites

None. Training is available to every signed-in user, and your progress is tracked separately for you - it does not depend on roles or claims.

## Browse the curriculum and complete a lesson

**To complete a lesson**

1. On the navigation pane, select **Training**.

    ![Training home page, showing the Getting Started with CluedIn category with two modules and their completion progress]({{ "/assets/images/get-started/training-hub/training-home.png" | relative_url }})

1. Select a module to open it.

1. In the lesson list, select a lesson. Required lessons are marked **Required**; the rest are marked **Optional** and are never needed to complete the module.

    ![Module page showing the lesson list with required and optional lessons, and a video lesson playing]({{ "/assets/images/get-started/training-hub/module-page.png" | relative_url }})

1. For a video lesson, watch the video. For a guide lesson, read the article shown on the page.

1. Select **Mark as complete**.

## Verify the result

The lesson's row shows a checkmark, and the module's progress bar on the Training home page updates to reflect it. This is saved against your user, so it is still there after you refresh the page or sign out and back in.

## What this changes

Completing a lesson only updates your own progress - it never changes golden records or other data. Once every required lesson in every module a certification requires is complete, that certification is awarded automatically; you do not need to request it.

## View your earned certifications

**To view your certifications**

1. On the Training home page, select **View certifications**.

1. Certifications you have earned show the date you earned them. Certifications you have not earned yet list which modules are still outstanding.

    ![Certifications page showing an earned certification with its award date]({{ "/assets/images/get-started/training-hub/certifications-page.png" | relative_url }})

## If something goes wrong

If a lesson you just completed still shows as incomplete after a few seconds, refresh the page - your progress was still saved, since it is written to the server before the page updates.
