## Learning outcome

Define architecture responsibilities, environment boundaries, evidence requirements, and promotion expectations before changing platform behavior.

## Scenario

Your team needs to change mappings, rules, and downstream behavior while stewards continue operating daily quality workflows. You need a working agreement that prevents experimentation from becoming production risk.

## Read

- [Built-in AI agents](/management/ai-agents/built-in-ai-agents)

## Exercise

1. Define what the Data Architect owns and what the Data Steward owns.
2. Define permitted work in development, test, and production.
3. Specify the evidence required before accepting an architectural change request.
4. Define who approves mapping, identity, rule, automation, and stream changes.
5. Create an architecture decision-log template with assumptions, alternatives, expected behavior, validation, and rollback considerations.

## Deliverable

An environment and responsibility policy plus an architecture decision-log template.

## Complete when

- Role boundaries are explicit.
- Development, test, and production have distinct purposes.
- Architectural changes require reproducible evidence and validation criteria.
