## Learning outcome

Use AI Agents as a design aid for the initial architect-owned rule set while keeping rule intent, validation, and activation under explicit human control.

## Scenario

The domain needs several initial rules and AI can help draft or suggest rule logic. Your responsibility is to use that assistance to accelerate architecture work without turning the Architect into the approver of day-to-day AI remediation suggestions.

## Read

- [Built-in AI agents](/management/ai-agents/built-in-ai-agents)
- [Create rules](/getting-started/rule-builder)

## Exercise

1. Choose one rule requirement from the domain architecture.
2. Use the relevant AI capability to help formulate or draft the rule logic.
3. Review the proposed logic against the business requirement and the modeled vocabulary.
4. Translate the accepted design into an architect-owned rule.
5. Test the rule on a representative non-production sample and inspect the resulting records.
6. Record what the AI suggested, what you changed, and why the final rule is acceptable.

Do not use this module to approve operational AI remediation suggestions or to turn enrichers on. Those activities belong to operational stewardship or another explicitly assigned owner.

## Deliverable

An AI-assisted rule design record containing the original requirement, AI contribution, architect changes, final rule, and validation evidence.

## Complete when

- AI assistance is traceable and does not replace architectural judgment.
- The final rule is owned and validated as part of the platform architecture.
- No operational remediation or enrichment approval is performed by the Architect.
