Besides the written guides in this section, CluedIn also includes a short, interactive product tour that you can take directly in the application. It gives you a quick overview of the major areas of CluedIn before you dive into the step-by-step guides.

## What the tour covers

The tour is a 13-step guided walkthrough covering:

- Welcome and prerequisites
- Core concepts
- First-time setup (connecting data sources and ingesting data)
- Data modeling and business domains
- Rules and processing
- Deduplication projects
- Streams and exports
- Tag monitoring
- Workflows
- Clean projects
- Hierarchy builder
- AI agents and agent jobs
- Where to find documentation and support

Each step gives a short explanation, and most steps include a **Take me there** button that navigates you directly to that area of CluedIn.

## Starting the tour

The tour opens automatically the first time you sign in to a new organization. You can dismiss it at any time with **Skip tour** or the close button — it never blocks you from using the rest of CluedIn.

**To open the tour again at any time**

1. In the top navigation bar, select the **Help** (**?**) icon.
1. Select **Product Tour**.

    ![Product Tour entry in the Help menu]({{ "/assets/images/getting-started/product-tour/help-menu-product-tour.png" | relative_url }})

1. Depending on your progress, select **Start tour**, **Resume tour**, or **Restart tour**.

    ![Resume and restart options]({{ "/assets/images/getting-started/product-tour/resume-restart.png" | relative_url }})

## Skip, resume, and restart

- **Skip** closes the tour without losing your place — your progress is saved so you can pick up later.
- **Resume** reopens the tour at the exact step you last viewed.
- **Restart** clears your progress and reopens the tour from the first step, even if you already completed it.

Once you finish the last step, the tour won't reappear automatically — you can always relaunch it from the Help menu as described above.
