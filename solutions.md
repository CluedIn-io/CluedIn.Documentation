# Solutions & integrations

How does CluedIn fit with the rest of my stack?
  Solution architects and system integrators.

Guides for connecting CluedIn to specific ecosystems, and worked use cases showing how the platform is applied to a business problem.

These guides link to the product documentation rather than repeating it.

## In this section

- [Microsoft Purview](https://documentation.cluedin.net/solutions/purview.md): CluedIn and Purview cover different halves of the same problem: Purview catalogues and governs metadata, CluedIn masters the data itself.
- [Power Apps](https://documentation.cluedin.net/solutions/power-apps.md): Surfacing CluedIn golden records inside Power Apps and Dataverse, and pushing changes made there back into CluedIn.
- [Power Automate](https://documentation.cluedin.net/solutions/power-automate.md): Triggering Power Automate flows from CluedIn, and calling CluedIn from a flow.
- [Microsoft Fabric](https://documentation.cluedin.net/solutions/fabric.md): Moving data between CluedIn and Fabric in both directions, and running CluedIn rules against data that lives in Fabric.
- [Azure Data Factory](https://documentation.cluedin.net/solutions/azure-data-factory.md): Using Data Factory pipelines to move data into and out of CluedIn.
- [Master Data Services](https://documentation.cluedin.net/solutions/master-data-services.md): Connecting CluedIn to SQL Server Master Data Services, either directly or through Azure Relay.
- [Copilot](https://documentation.cluedin.net/solutions/copilot.md): Asking questions about your golden records in natural language through Copilot.
- [Event Hub Integration](https://documentation.cluedin.net/solutions/event-hub-integration.md) (how-to): Azure Event Hub integration enables the transmission of workflow events from CluedIn to your event hub.
- [Azure Open AI Integration](https://documentation.cluedin.net/solutions/open-ai-integration.md) (how-to): With Azure OpenAI integration, you can leverage AI capabilities to analyze data and determine mapping for you.
- [Excel Add-in](https://documentation.cluedin.net/solutions/excel-add-in.md) (how-to): In this article, you will learn how to add the Excel Add-in, connect it to CluedIn, and use it to work with data.
- [Use cases](https://documentation.cluedin.net/solutions/use-cases.md): End-to-end walkthroughs of a business problem: what the data looks like going in, what CluedIn does to it, and what comes out the other side.
