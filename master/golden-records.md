# Golden records

How to work with golden records once they exist: see what they are connected to, read their history, understand why a value was chosen, and remove records or individual data parts.

For what a golden record is, see [Core concepts](https://documentation.cluedin.net/get-started/core-concepts/golden-records.md).

## In this section

- [Golden record relations](https://documentation.cluedin.net/master/golden-records/golden-record-relations.md) (how-to): Relations connect golden records by linking a source record to a target record through a specific edge type (for example, /WorksFor or /FinancedBy).
- [History](https://documentation.cluedin.net/master/golden-records/history.md) (concept): History is an important aspect of a golden record because it shows which data parts make up a golden record, what values each data part has, and what changes were made to those data parts.
- [Explain Log](https://documentation.cluedin.net/master/golden-records/explain-log.md) (concept): In this article, you will learn how to use the Explain Log to track the sequence of processing steps and rules that shape a golden record.
- [Delete golden records](https://documentation.cluedin.net/master/golden-records/delete-golden-records.md) (how-to): In this article, you will learn how to delete golden records.
- [Delete individual data parts](https://documentation.cluedin.net/master/golden-records/delete-data-parts-from-golden-records.md) (how-to): In this article, you will learn how to delete specific data parts (also referred to as records) from a golden record.
- [How to roll back or undo changes](https://documentation.cluedin.net/master/golden-records/how-to-rollback-or-undo.md) (how-to): Mistakes happen — whether it’s applying a transformation you didn’t mean to, merging the wrong records, or running a cleaning project with unintended results.
- [Bulk actions on the search results page](https://documentation.cluedin.net/master/golden-records/bulk-actions-on-search-results.md) (how-to): In this article, you will learn how to select multiple golden records on the search results page and perform a bulk action on them: delete, merge, reprocess, split, or add a tag.
